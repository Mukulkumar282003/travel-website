About Switzerland

Switzerland is a beautiful European country known for its snow-covered mountains, peaceful lakes, green valleys and charming villages.

Top Attractions

Interlaken, Lucerne, Zurich, Zermatt and Jungfraujoch.

Things to Experience

Enjoy mountain views, scenic train journeys, lake visits and beautiful nature walks.

Best Time to Visit

June to September for pleasant weather and December to February for snow activities.
