About Bali

Bali is a beautiful Indonesian island famous for its beaches, temples, green rice fields and natural beauty. It is a wonderful destination for travellers.

Top Attractions

Ubud, Kuta Beach, Tanah Lot Temple and Nusa Penida.

Things to Experience

Enjoy beautiful beaches, explore local markets, visit temples and experience traditional Balinese culture.

Best Time to Visit

April to October.
